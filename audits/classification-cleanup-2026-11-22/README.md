# Practical classification cleanup

Reviewed the 527 deferred records from the published November 21 queue against
existing evidence and approved vocabulary definitions. No source descriptions,
Wikidata statements, catalog admission, or taxonomy terms were changed.

86 superseding reviews are accepted: 48 jobs, 27 resources, and 11 software entries.
All previously supported assignments are preserved. Clear resource subject wording
supports domain assignments; the two ISO language-code records already had the
correct domain. The complete sales role supports Life Sciences, and the LegalTech
role maps to the existing Government & Public Sector definition, which includes law.
GraphScope gains graph analytics and Turtle Editor Viewer gains graph visualization.

An excerpt ending in an ellipsis is not automatically an unresolved review when
its role-specific statements already support a decision. Conversely, job excerpts
containing only company introductions remain deferred. Software with a clear
function outside a single approved primary type can retain supported tags without
forcing a type. No unknown record was converted to reviewed-empty.

441 records remain deferred: 396 resources, 38 jobs, and 7 software entries.
Of these, 65 have identity/admission questions; 376 need descriptive evidence.
The machine-readable review.json records every cohort member, disposition, exact
evidence path, prior review and superseding review (where applicable). Identity
questions are tracked separately, but remain visibly deferred in the automated
inbox until resolved; this cleanup does not suppress queue entries.

The accepted review log must be applied and verified through the normal publisher.
Local validation is not proof of live publication. Issue #87 stays open pending
publication reconciliation and resolution of the remaining findings.
